# Portfolio Redesign Domain Glossary

Canonical terminology and mental models for Kedar Mulay's portfolio redesign and project curation.

## Core Design Philosophy

**Radical Simplicity ("Make It Click")**:
The core design and communication principle: explaining complex systems, backend architectures, and research ideas in plain, neutral, grounded language so that anyone immediately understands them without buzzwords or academic jargon.
_Avoid_: Intellectual jargon, Pretentious design speak, Buzzword salad

**Universal Case Study Flow**:
The consistent 4-step narrative structure applied to all projects:
1. **Problem / Idea**: What was broken or what question was being explored.
2. **Plan & Scope**: How the work was framed and bounded.
3. **Design & Implementation**: How the interface, flows, and mechanics actually work.
4. **Outcome**: The real results, learnings, or impact.
_Avoid_: Inconsistent case study layouts, Methodology overload

## Project Portfolio & Work Types

**Selected Work**:
The curated showcase of primary case studies on the homepage, presenting commercial systems, workflow architecture, and high-impact prototypes under a unified visual flow with clear contextual metadata.
_Avoid_: Clunky separate sub-pages, Rigid academic partitions

**Two-Layer Tag Hierarchy**:
The structured metadata model defining project context:
`[Origin: academic | client | research | speculative] / [Project Type] / [Project Name]`.
_Avoid_: Flat uninformative tags, Inconsistent taxonomy

## Posture & Roles

**Systems & Interaction Designer**:
Kedar's professional title, reflecting end-to-end capability across complex backend/system user flows and tactile, polished interface craft.
_Avoid_: Generalist Designer, UI/UX Designer, Freelancer

**Design Partner**:
The peer-level relationship model for working directly with founders and technical teams on core product and systems problems.
_Avoid_: Commodity contractor, Ticket taker
