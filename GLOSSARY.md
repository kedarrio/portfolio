# Portfolio Redesign Domain Glossary

Core domain vocabulary for Kedar Mulay's portfolio redesign, commercial positioning, and content architecture.

## Language

**Design Partner**:
A strategic, lifecycle collaborator who embeds with technical founders and high-end product teams to architect complex systems and craft core digital experiences.
_Avoid_: Freelancer, contractor, hired gun, agency vendor, gig worker

**Systems & Interaction Designer**:
The canonical title and professional discipline defining Kedar's practice across architectural user flows, backend state alignment, and tactile computational interfaces.
_Avoid_: UI/UX designer, graphic designer, product designer (generic), Figma pusher

**Context Design**:
The emerging discipline of architecting how AI models and systems ingest operational context and augment human creativity while preserving human agency and authentic subjective experience.
_Avoid_: Prompt engineering, AI wrapper design, chatbot styling

**Speculative Inquiry**:
Applied futures research and tangible computational probes that derisk emerging technological paradigms and serve as zero-to-one R&D for commercial software.
_Avoid_: School project, academic assignment, student work, theoretical demo
